Your battery, impossible to miss

A huge battery indicator you can read from across the room — on the screen, on the lock screen, in the Dynamic Island and in widgets.

Coming soon to the App Store Write to me to get notified about the release

iPhone · iOS 17+

Features

🔋

Huge and readable

The battery fills almost the whole screen and grows even bigger when you turn the phone sideways.

🔒

Lock screen & Dynamic Island

A Live Activity shows the charge on the lock screen and updates in real time while charging.

🧩

Widgets

Round, rectangular and inline lock screen widgets, plus small and medium Home Screen widgets.

🕰️

Big clock

An optional large clock above the battery — perfect as a bedside display while charging.

🔔

Charge alerts

Get a sound alert when the charge reaches the level you choose, with several melodies to pick from.

🎨

Colors that mean something

Green while charging, yellow below 40%, orange below 20%, red below 10% — or monochrome and accent styles.
