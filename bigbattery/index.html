Your battery, impossible to miss

A huge charge display you can read from across the room — on the screen, in Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets and in StandBy.

iPhone · iOS 17+

Features

🔋

Huge and readable

The battery fills the screen and gets even bigger when you turn the phone sideways.

🧩

Widgets and StandBy

Circular, rectangular and inline Lock Screen widgets, small and medium Home Screen widgets. They update on their own and show up in StandBy while charging.

🕰️

Big clock

A large clock above the battery turns your iPhone into a nightstand display while it charges.

🔔

Charge alert

Pick a level from 50 to 100% and one of five melodies to know when your phone is charged.

🎯

Precise to the percent

iOS reports the battery to apps in 5% steps, so BigBattery estimates the exact percentage from your charging speed.

☀️

Screen on while charging

The screen stays awake on the charger and locks as usual when you unplug. Low Power Mode is one shortcut away.

🎨

Colors that mean something

Color by charge level, monochrome or accent — plus a charging animation you notice at a glance.

🔒

Free and private

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. BigBattery collects no data.
