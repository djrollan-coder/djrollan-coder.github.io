What it does

🗂️

Two panes, keyboard first

The classic layout: F3–F8, tabs, pinned favourites, type-ahead search and a clickable path. Copy and move go from one pane to the other without a dialog in the way.

⚡

Fast operations

Copying and moving show speed, progress and a queue. ⌘Z undoes the last one, and you are warned before a transfer fills the disk.

✏️

Batch rename

Counters, search and replace, case and extension changes — with a preview of the result before anything is written.

🔍

Duplicates and archives

Find duplicate files by content and clear them out. Open a zip archive like a folder, pull single files out of it, and pack new ones.

🎬

Preview as you browse

Quick Look on Space, a built-in audio and video player with playlists, and image conversion between JPEG, PNG and HEIF.

🎨

Made your own

Skins, individual colours, fonts, list modes, coloured tabs, and CPU and memory readouts in the toolbar. Plus a disk check that measures a drive's link and its sustained read and write speed.

FAQ

Why does it ask for access to a folder?

The App Store version runs in Apple's sandbox, which gives an app no files of its own accord. On the first launch you grant a folder or a disk through the open panel; the grant covers everything inside it and is remembered, so you are not asked again.

Which archives does it handle?

ZIP, both ways: you can browse one without unpacking it, drag single files out, and create new archives. RAR, 7z and tar are not supported in this version.

Is there a built-in terminal?

Not in the App Store version — the sandbox does not allow an app to start a shell. The toolbar button opens your own Terminal or iTerm at the folder you are looking at.

Can it connect to network shares or FTP?

No. Mounting network volumes is something the sandbox refuses, so the App Store version works with local disks, external drives and cloud folders that are already mounted on your Mac — iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Dropbox and the like.

Does it send anything anywhere?

No. It has no analytics, no accounts and no network calls of its own. Details are in the privacy policy.

I want a refund

Purchases go through Apple, so refunds do too — use reportaproblem.apple.com. If something is broken, write to me first: it may be quicker to fix than to refund.
