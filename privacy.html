Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Lingrab is developed by Roman Motorniy (“we”). This policy explains what data Lingrab handles. In short: your screen content and text never leave your Mac.

Data processed on your Mac only

  • Screen capture. When you select an area, Lingrab captures only that area to recognize text using Apple’s on-device Vision framework. Images are not saved or transmitted.
  • Translation. Translation uses Apple’s built-in Translation framework. Lingrab does not send your text to us or to third parties.
  • Clipboard history. Copied text is stored locally in your user preferences so you can copy it again. You can clear it at any time. Items marked as concealed by password managers are not stored.
  • Keyboard input. To fix the wrong keyboard layout, Lingrab looks at the current word you type and checks it against macOS dictionaries. Keystrokes are not logged, stored or transmitted.

Data sent over the network

The only network requests Lingrab makes are license checks. When you activate Pro, your license key and your Mac’s name are sent to our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy, to verify the purchase and limit activations. The key is re-checked about once a week.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. We receive your name, email and order details to provide support and send your license key. We never see your full card details.

Analytics and tracking

Lingrab contains no analytics, advertising or tracking. This website does not use cookies; your language choice is stored only in your browser.

Your rights

You can ask us to access or delete data related to your purchase by writing to djrollan1@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
