How to see the exact battery percentage on iPhone

By Roman MotornyiNovember 20, 20262 min readЧитать на русском

The battery icon tells you roughly how much charge is left. When you want the number — and want it big enough to read from the bed while the phone charges — here is where to look.

Turn on the percentage in the status bar

On iPhones with Face ID, open Settings → Battery and turn on Battery Percentage. The number then appears inside the battery icon in the top-right corner. On iPhones with a Home button the percentage sits next to the icon.

Two other quick places:

  • Control Center — swipe down from the top-right corner; the percentage is shown at the top.
  • Batteries widget — add it to the Home Screen or Today View to see your iPhone, AirPods and Apple Watch at once.

Why apps only see 5% steps

iOS gives third-party apps the battery level rounded to 5%: an app sees 80%, then 85%, but not 82%. That is why many battery widgets jump in steps.

BigBattery works around it: it measures how fast your phone is charging and estimates the exact percentage between the steps, so the number on its screen moves one percent at a time.

A charge level you can read from across the room

BigBattery turns the whole screen into a battery indicator. Turn the phone sideways and it gets even bigger. While the phone charges it can:

  • show a big clock above the battery,
  • keep the screen awake on the charger and lock it as usual when you unplug,
  • play an alert at a level you choose, from 50 to 100%, with one of five melodies,
  • color the battery by charge level, or keep it monochrome or in an accent color.

Its Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets update on their own and show up in StandBy while the phone charges.

Keep the battery healthy

In Settings → Battery → Charging on iPhone 15 and later you can limit charging, for example to 80%, and Optimized Battery Charging is available on every recent iPhone. A charge alert in BigBattery helps if you prefer to unplug at a level yourself.

Questions

Does BigBattery work without an account?

Yes. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and it collects no data.

Which iPhones does it support?

Any iPhone with iOS 17 or later. It is free on the App Store.
