About me

I'm Roman Motornyi, an independent developer and DJ. I build and support the apps on this site, and I answer the support email myself.

What I make

Small native apps for Mac and iPhone that do one job well and stay out of the way:

  • Lingrab — copy text from anywhere on your Mac screen, read it aloud, fix the wrong keyboard layout and keep a safe clipboard history. On the Mac App Store.
  • BigBattery — a huge, readable charge display for iPhone, with widgets, StandBy and a charge alert. On the App Store.
  • All Commander — a fast dual-pane file manager for Mac in the spirit of Total Commander. Coming soon.
  • All Radio — radio stations from all over the world for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Coming soon.
  • DJ Pro 4 iRoll — a DJ app for Mac that works with Denon DJ gear. In development.

How I build them

  • Private by default. No accounts, no analytics, no ads. Text recognition, translation and speech run on your device.
  • Native. Written in Swift with Apple's own frameworks, so the apps feel at home and stay light.
  • Honest pages. What a page says an app does is what the app does today. If something changes, the page changes too.

Get in touch
