What it does

🌍

Stations from everywhere

Browse by genre and sub-genre, filter by name or country, and sort by what is best, most popular or highest quality.

✨

Search by vibe

Describe a mood, such as "deep chill night", and get matching stations. The player also suggests stations that feel like the one you are listening to.

🎵

See what is playing

When a station sends the song name, you see the artist, the title and the cover. Keep the songs you like as a list, sorted into folders by genre.

⭐

Your favourites

Star genres and like stations to keep them on one page, in the order you choose.

🚗

Travel mode

A longer buffer keeps the music playing through tunnels and short signal drops. There is a sleep timer too.

🎙️

Siri and AirPlay

Start a station by voice or from Shortcuts, send it to a speaker with AirPlay, and control playback from the lock screen.

FAQ

A station does not play. What is wrong?

Stations are run by their owners, and each one streams from its own server. A station can be offline or may have moved its stream. Try another one, and write to me if many stations fail at once.

Why is there no song name on some stations?

The song name comes from the station itself, and not every station sends it. A cover is shown only when one can be found for that name.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no registration. Your favourites, saved songs and settings stay on your device.

Can I record a station or download songs?

No. All Radio only plays the live stream. A saved song is just its name, not the audio.

My favourite station is missing.

The list comes from Radio Browser, an open directory that anyone can add a station to. New stations appear in the app after they are added there.

Does it send anything anywhere?

Only what it needs to work: requests to the station directory, to the station you play, and song names to look up covers. There are no analytics and no advertising. Details are in the privacy policy.

I want a refund

Purchases go through Apple, so refunds do too — use reportaproblem.apple.com. If something is broken, write to me first: it may be quicker to fix than to refund.
