Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

All Radio (“the app”) is developed by Roman Motornyi (“we”). We do not collect your personal data: the app has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and we run no server that receives your data.

On your device

Your favorites, saved songs, recently played stations and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Services the app connects to

To work, the app connects directly to the services below. Like any internet connection, each request shows your IP address to that service. Their own privacy policies apply.

  • Radio Browser (radio-browser.info), the public station directory: receives your searches, the genres you open, and a count when you start a station.
  • Radio stations: the audio comes directly from the server of the station you play.
  • Apple iTunes Search and Deezer: receive the name of the song that is playing, to find its cover.
  • Station logos and website links: logos load from wherever each station hosts them. A website link opens in your browser only if you tap it.

Siri and Shortcuts

If you use Siri or Shortcuts with the app, your requests are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy. We do not receive them.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data, because we do not have it.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. Stations are run by third parties, and we do not control what they broadcast.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
