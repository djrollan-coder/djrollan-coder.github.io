Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

All Radio & DJ Live (“the app”) is developed by Roman Motornyi (“we”). This policy explains what data the app collects and why.

Listening to radio

You can listen to radio stations without an account. Station lists come from the public Radio Browser directory; audio is streamed directly from each station’s server. Your favorites, saved tracks, alarms and settings are stored on your device.

Accounts

To follow DJs, broadcast or report content you create an account. We store your email address, display name, profile picture (optional), the DJs you follow and your broadcast history. Your password is stored only as a secure hash.

Live broadcasts

When you go live, the app uses the microphone or audio input and, for video broadcasts, the camera. The stream is sent to our server and delivered to listeners in real time. Broadcasts are not recorded unless the app clearly says so. Camera and microphone are used only while you are broadcasting.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store a push notification token to tell you when DJs you follow go live. You can turn this off in iOS Settings.

Moderation

Reports you submit and blocks you set are stored to keep the community safe. We may remove content or accounts that violate the rules.

Spotify export

Exporting saved tracks opens Spotify; we do not receive your Spotify credentials.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and do not use third-party advertising or analytics. Data is shared only with service providers that host our servers, or when required by law.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app (My Account → Delete account). This permanently removes your profile and associated data from our server. You can also email djrollan1@gmail.com.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
