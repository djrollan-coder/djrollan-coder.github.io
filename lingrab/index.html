FAQ

How do I install Lingrab?

Get Lingrab free from the Mac App Store and open it — the icon appears in the menu bar. On the first capture macOS asks for the Screen Recording permission: Lingrab needs it only to read the area you select.

How do I get Pro?

Pro is a one-time in-app purchase in Lingrab from the Mac App Store. It stays yours on every Mac that uses the same Apple ID.

Does Lingrab send my screen anywhere?

No. Text recognition, translation and speech run entirely on your Mac. There are no accounts, no tracking and no ads.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases go through Apple, so refunds do too — use reportaproblem.apple.com. If something is broken, write to me first: it may be quicker to fix.
