FAQ

How do I install Lingrab?

Download the zip, move Lingrab to Applications and open it. macOS will ask for Accessibility and Screen Recording permissions — they are needed to detect ⌘ + drag and read the selected area.

How do I get Pro?

Pro will be available in the Mac App Store version of Lingrab as a one-time in-app purchase. The free version from this site keeps working without any limits in time.

Does Lingrab send my screen anywhere?

No. Text recognition and translation happen on your Mac. The only network request is the license key check.
