Refund Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Lingrab is free on the Mac App Store. Lingrab Pro is a one-time in-app purchase made through Apple.

How to request a refund

Apple handles all payments for the Mac App Store, so refunds go through Apple as well. Open reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find Lingrab Pro and choose “Request a refund”. Apple decides on the request and returns the money to your original payment method.

Something not working?

Write to me first at djrollan1@gmail.com — it may be quicker to fix the problem than to refund.

After a refund

Pro features are turned off and Lingrab keeps working as the free version.
