Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Lingrab is developed by Roman Motornyi (“we”). This policy explains what data Lingrab handles. In short: Lingrab collects no data, and your screen content and text never leave your Mac.

Data processed on your Mac only

  • Screen capture. When you select an area, Lingrab captures only that area to recognize text using Apple’s on-device Vision framework. It needs the macOS Screen Recording permission only for this. Images are not saved or transmitted.
  • Translation (Pro). Translation uses Apple’s built-in Translation framework on your Mac. Lingrab does not send your text to us or to third parties. macOS may download translation languages from Apple the first time you use them.
  • Read aloud. Speech uses the voices built into macOS and runs on your Mac.
  • Layout fix. When you choose Lingrab’s command in the Services menu, macOS passes it the text you selected; Lingrab converts it and hands it back. Lingrab does not watch your typing.
  • Clipboard history. Copied text is stored locally on your Mac so you can copy it again. You can clear it at any time. Lingrab never stores items marked as concealed by password managers, crypto wallet addresses and recovery phrases, private keys or API keys.

Network

Lingrab does not send your screen, your text or any information about you to us or to third parties.

Purchases

Lingrab Pro is sold as an in-app purchase through the Mac App Store. Apple processes the payment; we do not receive your name, email or payment details. Apple’s handling of your data is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Analytics and tracking

Lingrab contains no analytics, advertising or tracking. This website does not use cookies; your language choice is stored only in your browser.

Contact
